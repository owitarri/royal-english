Mission 5 · Grammar⏱ 7 min

The King lives in London

With he, she, it (the King, Charlotte, the dog) we add -s: The King lives. Charlotte goes.
With I, you, we, they (the guards, the corgis) there is no -s: The guards wear. I love.
Он, она, оно (один человек) — добавляем -s. Я, ты, мы, они (много людей) — без -s.

Mission 6 · Reading⏱ 12 min

Welcome to Buckingham Palace

Read the text. You can tap a paragraph to hear it, or listen to the whole text. Then answer the questions.

Прочитай текст. Можно нажать на абзац, чтобы его послушать. Потом ответь на вопросы.

Buckingham Palace is in London. It is the King's home and office in London. The palace is very, very big. It has 775 rooms! There are bedrooms, offices, and even a post office and a cinema.

Behind the palace there is a big garden. It is the biggest private garden in London. In May, the King has big garden parties there.

How do you know if the King is at home? Look at the flag on the roof! When the King is in the palace, you can see the Royal Standard. It is red, gold and blue. When he is not there, you can see the Union Flag.

In front of the palace you can see the guards. They wear red jackets and tall black hats. The hats are called bearskins. On some days, at 11 o'clock in the morning, there is the Changing of the Guard. Many tourists come to watch it. The guards do not smile and do not talk when they are working.

In summer, people can visit some rooms of the palace. These rooms are called the State Rooms.

Text 2 · The Queen who loved corgis

This text is about the past, so we say was, had, lived. Read and find these words!

Этот текст о прошлом: was (была), had (имела), lived (жили). Найди эти слова в тексте.

Queen Elizabeth II was the Queen for 70 years, from 1952 to 2022. That is longer than any other British king or queen. She was King Charles's mother.

The Queen loved animals. Her favourite animals were dogs and horses. Her favourite dogs were corgis. Corgis are small dogs with short legs and big ears.

Her first corgi was called Susan. Susan was a present for her 18th birthday. In her life the Queen had more than 30 corgis! The corgis lived in the palace with her and often travelled with her.
