Mission 4 · Reading⏱ 10 min

The King's busy day

Read the text. Tap a paragraph to hear it. Then do the tasks.

Прочитай текст. Нажми на абзац, чтобы послушать. Потом выполни задания.

What does the King do every day? Royal writers say that King Charles has a very busy day.

The King gets up early. For breakfast he has fruit, seeds and a cup of tea. He likes honey too.

After breakfast the King reads his letters and papers. Every day he gets special red boxes with important papers from the government. Then he has meetings and visits people all over the country.

And lunch? The King doesn't eat lunch! Royal writers say lunch takes too much time for him.

At five o'clock the King has afternoon tea with Queen Camilla. They have sandwiches and cake. Tea time is very important for them!

After tea the King works again. He has dinner at half past eight. Then he works at his desk until late at night. Sometimes he goes to bed after midnight! Queen Camilla says he works too much.

Mission 5 · Grammar⏱ 8 min

The King doesn't eat lunch

I / you / we / they → don't: I don't live in a palace.
he / she / it → doesn't: The King doesn't eat lunch. (после doesn't — без -s: doesn't eat, НЕ doesn't eats)
Questions: Do you like tea? Does the King like tea? → Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.

Choose don't or doesn't.

Short answers
